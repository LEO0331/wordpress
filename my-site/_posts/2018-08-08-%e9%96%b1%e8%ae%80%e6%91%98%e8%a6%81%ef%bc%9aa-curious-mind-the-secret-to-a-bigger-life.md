簡介：作者藉由好奇心對話訪問了許許多多知名人士，並於問問題及聊天的過程中發現意想不到的電影題材和點子，以自身為例，說明好奇心的重要性，不但能夠點燃熱忱，更能從不同的觀點中，找尋生命的意義。

心得：我很佩服作者有勇氣及下定決心透過各種管道去聯繫自己想要深入了解的人，這種單純對人的好奇心化為行動力，使作者能從各式各樣的角度看世界，成為一個有故事的人，創作的想法也源源不絕的冒出，透過追尋內在的好奇心，找回與人最真誠的對話。
<p style="text-align: center">關於<span style="color: #ff0000">好奇心對話</span>的兩三句話…</p>

<ol>
 	<li>We are living in the golden age of curiosity, because we have the freedom.</li>
 	<li>Life isn't about finding answers, its about asking questions.</li>
 	<li>Curiosity is the path to freedom itself.</li>
 	<li>Take a resolution and turn it into a habit.</li>
 	<li>Ideas and decisions winging all day long without rules or rigidity.</li>
 	<li>Look at the same city from completely different perspectives.</li>
 	<li>The big stuff is different-goals values priorities.</li>
 	<li>Thinking like other people.</li>
 	<li>Curiosity spurs me to chase my passions.</li>
 	<li>Pitch ideas, strong stomach to absorb rejection and exuded a barrier.</li>
 	<li>Calmly separate yourself from what is being done to you.</li>
 	<li>Perfectly crystalize something in a way I will never forget.</li>
 	<li>Manners won't about the rules, they were about grace and hospitality.</li>
 	<li>Shape the story, use curiosity to instill confidence, recontextualize movie and pitch movies.</li>
 	<li>Movie producer ask: what story are we telling? what kind of movie is...? what felling are we trying to convey?</li>
 	<li>Keep the nervousness at bay: work on the talk few days before not too early (avoid worry everyday).</li>
 	<li>Ask questions:whats the talk supposed to be? whats the best possible vision? what do people coming to this event expect or want to hear? who is the audience? tell stories but not lectures.</li>
 	<li>Introduction for conversation: this is not business with...and I don't want a job, but I would like to meet xxx for 5 mins to talk to him/her.</li>
 	<li>Curiosity will conquer fear even more than bravery will.</li>
 	<li>As it turns out, being different requires courage.</li>
 	<li>Ask for help rather than direct it, use questions instead of marching order and transmit values.</li>
 	<li>What are we missing? Marriages drifted into disconnection helped by revival of genuine curiosity.</li>
 	<li>Religion: the opium of the masses, religion was designed to provide enough answers that people stopped asking questions.</li>
 	<li>Internet anesthetize us instead of inspire us.</li>
 	<li>Invest a little bit of effort across a wide range of time space and people confident that its the right thing todo.</li>
 	<li>A name can trigger a wave of memories and each person is an adventure.</li>
 	<li>I have been curious about your work, I am trying to broaden my sense of that world, and i was wondering if you would be willing to spend 20mins talking to me about what u do, what the challenges and satisfactions are:send brief follow up, grateful email highlighting one story or point that you enjoyed.</li>
 	<li>All of these sad goodbyes are bolstered by the reunion with your family and friends you have pictured in your head since leaving in the first place.</li>
</ol>
