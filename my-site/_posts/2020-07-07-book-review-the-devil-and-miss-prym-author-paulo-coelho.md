Brief Introduction: The stranger, Carlos, enters the isolated mountain town of Viscos with the devil literally by his side. He proposes a wager to the town: if someone turns up murdered within a week, he will give the town enough gold to make everyone wealthy so that they could leave the town. Carlos chooses Chantal Prym to show her where the gold is, ensuring that townspeople believe in his words. He wants to vindicate that people are basically evil. Chantal has two choices: disappear with the gold sneaky or do nothing. However, the corrupt townspeople decide to sacrifice Berta, the widow who can see the hidden devil, to get the gold. Consequently, a battle for the town's souls has begun.
